# 🎨 Prompty AI do grafik promocyjnych Lectoro AI

Zestaw 4 profesjonalnych promptów (zoptymalizowanych pod Midjourney v6, DALL-E 3, Imagen 3 oraz FLUX) do wygenerowania grafik promocyjnych, banerów Chrome Web Store (Marquee 920x680, Promo Tile 440x280, Hero 1280x800) oraz materiałów marketingowych.

---

## 🎬 Prompt 1: Podwójne napisy wideo (YouTube & Netflix Bilingual Subtitles)
> **Zastosowanie:** Baner promocyjny funkcji wideo, zrzut ekranu do Chrome Web Store, prezentacja nakładki napisów.  
> **Format:** 16:9 (1920x1080 lub 1280x800)

```text
Cinematic 3D render of a futuristic sleek laptop screen playing a high-budget sci-fi movie in a dark cozy room with ambient neon blue and purple backlighting. Floating above the bottom of the video player are crisp, glowing bilingual subtitles in a modern translucent glassmorphic interface: the top line in English and the bottom line in translated glowing text. A subtle, glowing interactive UI cursor hovers over one highlighted word, revealing a floating mini AI tooltip with vocabulary meaning and pronunciation waveform. Premium tech aesthetic, dark mode UI, vibrant cyber-cyan and electric-violet glow, clean typography, soft depth of field, ultra-detailed 8k, Octane Render style, Unreal Engine 5 quality. --ar 16:9 --v 6.0 --style raw
```

## 🌐 Prompt 2: Inteligentne tłumaczenie tekstu na stronach WWW (AI Web Reader & Assistant)
> **Zastosowanie:** Prezentacja dymka AI, tłumaczenia zaznaczonego tekstu na dowolnym portalu internetowym.  
> **Format:** 16:9 lub 3:2

```text
An ultra-modern, aesthetic web browser interface floating in 3D isometric space against a deep dark navy background. The web page displays an elegant digital magazine article with glowing highlighted text. Hovering above the selected text is a stunning frosted glassmorphic popup card with rounded corners, subtle neon border gradient (electric purple to cyan), featuring an AI tutor breakdown: source word, phonetic transcription, natural context translation, and a glowing sound wave icon. Soft ambient volumetric lighting, floating 3D glass widgets, clean minimalism, Apple design language, sleek tech UI/UX mockup, high-end commercial digital art, 8k resolution. --ar 16:9 --v 6.0
```

> **Format:** 1:1 lub 4:3

```text
A dynamic composition of sleek 3D holographic flashcards floating in zero gravity over a minimalist dark studio background. Each card is made of frosted acrylic glass with glowing neon edges in emerald green, amber yellow, and violet. The main centered card displays a highlighted vocabulary word with audio wave icons, learning streak counter, and SRS rating buttons ('Again', 'Hard', 'Good', 'Easy') glowing at the bottom. Floating glowing particles and subtle 3D brain synapses in the background symbolizing memory retention and spaced repetition. Premium futuristic educational UI, isometric perspective, elegant render, Ray Traced reflections, 8k. --ar 16:9 --v 6.0
```
